*   What advice is given during ANC?
	The following advices are to be given:
	1)  **Diet** – Diet in pregnancy should be nutritious , balanced and with woman’s choice. She should eat adequately to fulfill the extra demand of the growing foetus. Birth weight is correlated to the weight gain of mother during pregnancy.
	2)  **Rest and sleep** – Hard and strenuous work should be avoided in first trimester and the last 4 weeks of pregnancy. Eight hours sleep at night and at least two hours rest after mid day meal is to be advised. In pregnancy lateral posture is comfortable.
	3)  **Bowel** – Regular intake of green leafy vegetables, fruits and extra fluids prevents constipation during pregnancy.
	4)  **Personal cleanliness** – Bath is to be taken daily and careful against slipping in the bathroom due to imbalance.
	5)  **Clothing, shoes and belts** – Loose and comfortable garments are advised. High heel shoes are better to be avoided in pregnancy.
	6)  **Dental care** – The dentist should be consulted at the earliest period if necessary. Oral hygiene is to be maintained carefully.
	7)  **Care of the breasts** – Nipple is to be cleaned. If nipples are retracted, correction is to be done by manipulation.
	8)  **Sexual activity** – If there is history of abortion or preterm labour sexual activity has to be restricted during first trimester and last six weeks.
	9)  **Travel** – Journey is to be avoided specially in the first trimester and last six weeks. Rail route is preferable to bus route. Air travel causes less risk but it is contraindicated in case of placenta praevia, PET ,severe anaemia and sickle cell disease.
	10) **Smoking and alcohol** – Smoking and alcohol is to be avoided because it may cause abortion, premature labour and IUGR.
	11) **Drug** – Medicine is to be prescribed cautiously because few drugs can cross the placenta and cause foetal abnormality.
	12) **Immunisation** – counseling about immunisation.
	13) **Mental preparation** – sufficient time and opportunity must be given to the expectant mothers to have a free and frank talk on all aspects of pregnancy and delivery.
	14) **Child care** – Mother has to be educated about the hygiene, rearing of the child and breast feeding.
	15) **Birth plan** – Mother is to be counseled about birth plan. High risk mother has to be delivered in an institution.
	16) **Warning sign**- The mother is to be warned to report about swelling of feet, convulsion, headache, blurring of vision, vaginal bleeding, discharge, less foetal movement and any other unusual symptoms.
	17) **Family Planning** – Mother has to be educated and motivated during the antenatal period for family planning. If she is multi parous, she should be motivated for puerperal sterilization.

	At the end of the antenatal visit she should be persuaded to attend antenatal clinic positively on the schedule date of visit.
*   What are the aims and objectives of ANC?
	* ==The aims== are: 
		1. To screen the 'high-risk' cases. 
		2. To prevent or to detect and treat at the earliest any complication.
		3. To ensure continued risk assessment and to provide ongoing primary preventive health care.
		4. To educate the mother about the physiology of pregnancy and labor by demonstrations, charts and diagrams (mothercraft classes), so that fear is removed and psychology is improved.
		5. To discuss with the couple about the place, time and mode of delivery, provisionally and care of the newborn.
		6. To motivate the couple about the need of family planning and also appropriate advice to couple seeking medical termination of pregnancy. 
	- ==The objective== is to ensure a normal pregnancy with delivery of a healthy baby from a healthy mother.

### Complications
*   What are the complications of the 1st stage of labour?
	* Prolonged 1st stage of labour 
	* Fetal distress
	* Early rupture of membrane with or without cord prolapse 
	* Hypotonic or incoordinate cord prolapse 
	* Cervical dystocia 
*   When is the 1st stage considered prolonged?
	* Duration >12 hours
	* Cervical dilatation 
		* Primi <1 cm/hr
		* Multi <1.5 cm/hr
	* Descent of presenting part 
		* Primi <1cm/hr
		* Multi <2cm/hr
* Prolonged 2nd stage  ^05d1ce
	* Duration 
		* Primi >2 hrs
		* Multi >1 hr
* **The labour is said to be prolonged if the duration of 1st & 2nd stage is more than the arbitrary limit of 18 hrs** 

*   What are the causes of prolonged 1st stage of labour?
	**Causes of Prolonged first stage**
	
	**1. Fault in Power**
		Abnormal uterine contraction such as uterine inertia (common) or incoordinate uterine contraction.
	
	**2. Fault in the Passage**
		a. Contracted pelvis
		b. Cervical dystocia
		c. Pelvic tumour
		d. Full bladder
	
	**3. Fault in the Passenger**
		a. Malposition (occipito-posterior position)
		b. Malpresentation (Face, Brow)
		c. Congenital anomalies of the fetus (Hydrocephalus).
	
	**4. Others**
		Injudicious (early) administration of sedatives and analgesics before the active labour begins.
*   If you don't manage a prolonged 1st stage, what will happen?
	**Complications of prolonged labour**
	*   **Fetal complications**
	    1. Hypoxia
	    2. Intrauterine infection, pneumonia
	    3. Intracranial stress or haemorrhage
	    4. Increased operative delivery
	
	*   **Maternal complications**
	    1. Maternal distress
	    2. Chorioamnionitis
	    3. Postpartum haemorrhage
	    4. Trauma to the genital tract (cervical tear, rupture uterus)
	    5. Increased operative delivery
	    6. Puerperal sepsis
	    7. Subinvolution
	    8. Increased maternal mortality & morbidity

	* See above

*   What are the events of the second stage?
	* Two phases 
		* Propulsive 
			* Start with full dilatation 
			* End with touching of fetal head with pelvic floor 
		* Expulsive phase
			* Start with bearing down effort 
			* End with delivery of the fetus 
	* Events
		* Full dilatation of cervix 
		* Increased Pain
		* Increased intensity & duration of uterine contractions 
		* Bearing down effort (starts from full dilatation of cervix)
		* Descent of fetus become more pronounced than 1st stage 
*   What is crowning?
	* After internal rotation of the head, further descent occurs until the subocciput lies underneath the pubic arch
	* At this stage, the maximum diameter of the head (biparietal diameter) stretches the vulval outlet without any recession of the head even after the contraction is over called "crowning of the head"

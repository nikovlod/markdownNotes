### **Practice Test 1: Structure and Written Expression (Part B)**

**16.** The main office of the factory can be found **(A) in** Maple Street **(B) in** New York City.
*   **Answer: (C)** (Note: The error is actually the preposition used for the street. It should be "on" Maple Street. The letters correspond to position in the sentence).

**17.** Because there are **(A) less** members present tonight **(B) than** there were last night, we must wait until the next meeting **(D) to vote**.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **fewer**. (Use "fewer" with count nouns like "members").

**18.** David is **(A) particularly** fond of cooking, and he **(B) often** cooks **(D) really** delicious meals.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **very**. ("Really" is too informal).

**19.** The **(A) progress** **(B) made** in space travel **(D) for** the early 1960s is remarkable.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **since**. (Use "since" with beginning time).

**20.** Sandra has **(A) not rarely** missed a **(B) play** or concert **(C) since** she was seventeen years old.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **rarely**. ("Not rarely" is a double negative).

**21.** The governor has not decided **(A) how** to deal with the new **(B) problems** **(D) already**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **yet**. (Use "yet" in negative sentences).

**22.** There was **(A) a** very interesting news **(C) on the** radio this morning **(D) about** the earthquake in Italy.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Remove **a**. ("News" is a non-count noun).

**23.** The professor had already given **(A) the** homework assignment when he had **(B) remembered** that Monday **(C) was** a holiday.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **remembered**. (Simple past tense is required here).

**24.** Having been **(A) beaten** by the police for striking an officer, the man **(C) will cry out** in pain.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **cried out**. (Sequence of tenses requires past tense).

**25.** This table is **(A) not** sturdy enough to support a television, and that one probably **(C) isn’t** **(D) neither**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **either**. (Negative agreement).

**26.** The bridge was **(A) hitting** by a large ship **(B) during** a sudden storm **(D) last week**.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **was hit**. (Passive voice).

**27.** The **(A) company** representative sold to **(B) the manager** a sewing machine **(C) for** forty dollars.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **the manager**. (Remove "to"—Subject + verb + indirect object + direct object).

**28.** The **(A) taxi** driver told the man to **(C) don’t** allow his disobedient son to hang out the window.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **not to allow**.

**29.** These televisions are **(A) quite** popular in Europe, but **(B) those** **(C) ones** are not.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **those**. (Remove "ones").

**30.** Harvey **(A) seldom** pays his bills on time, and his **(C) brother** **(D) does too**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **doesn't either**. ("Seldom" is negative).

**31.** The **(A) price** of crude oil used to be a **(B) great** deal **(C) lower** than now, **(D) wasn’t it**?
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **didn't it**. ("Used to" indicates past habit).

**32.** When **(A) an** university formulates **(B) new** regulations, it **(C) must** relay its decision to the students and faculty.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **a**. (University begins with a consonant sound "y").

**33.** Jim was **(A) upset** last night because he had to do too **(C) many** **(D) homeworks**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **much homework**. (Homework is non-count).

**34.** There **(A) is** some **(B) scissors** in the desk drawer in the bedroom if you need them.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **are**. ("Scissors" is plural).

**35.** The Board of Realtors doesn’t have any **(A) informations** about the **(B) increase** in rent for this area.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **information**. (Non-count noun).

**36.** George is not **(A) enough** intelligent to pass **(B) this** economics class without help.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **intelligent enough**. (Adjective + enough).

**37.** There were so **(A) much** people trying to leave the **(C) burning** building that the police had a great deal of trouble **(D) controlling** them.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **many**. ("People" is a count noun).

**38.** John lived **(A) in** New York **(B) since** 1960 to 1975, but he is **(C) now living** in Detroit.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **from**. (From time... to time).

**39.** The fire began **(A) in** the **(B) fifth** floor of the hotel, but it **(C) soon** spread to adjacent floors.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **on**. (On the floor of a building).

**40.** Mrs. Anderson bought **(A) last week a new sports car**; however, she has yet to learn how to operate the manual gearshift.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **a new sports car last week**. (Complement precedes modifier).

### **Practice Test 2: Structure and Written Expression (Part B)**

**16.** The officials object to **(A) them** wearing long dresses for the inaugural dance at the country club.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **their**. (Possessive adjective before a gerund).

**17.** Janet is finally used to **(A) cook** on an electric stove **(B) after having** a gas **(C) one** for so long.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **cooking**. (Be used to + verb + ing).

**18.** He **(A) knows to** repair the carburetor without **(B) taking** the whole **(C) car** apart.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **knows how**.

**19.** Stuart stopped **(A) to write** his letter because he had to **(B) leave** **(C) for** the hospital.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **writing**. (Stop + verb + ing).

**20.** She must **(A) retyping** the report **(B) before** she hands it in **(C) to** the director of financing.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **retype**. (Modal + simple form).

**21.** How **(A) much** times did Rick and Jennifer have to **(B) do** the experiment before they obtained the results they had **(D) been expecting**?
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **many**. ("Times" is a count noun).

**22.** Each of the **(A) students** in the accounting class has to type **(C) their** own research paper this semester.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **his**. ("Each" is singular).

**23.** Mrs. Stevens, along with her cousins from New Mexico, **(C) are** planning to attend the festivities.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **is**. (Subject "Mrs. Stevens" is singular).

**24.** They are going to have to leave soon, and **(D) so do we**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **so are we**. (Match the auxiliary "are").

**25.** All the students are looking **(C) forward spending** their free time relaxing in the sun this summer.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **forward to spending**.

**26.** Dresses, skirts, shoes, and children’s clothing are advertised **(A) at** **(B) great** reduced prices this weekend.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **greatly**. (Adverb + adjective).

**27.** Mary and her sister just bought two new **(C) winters** coats at the **(D) clearance** sale.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **winter**. (Noun acting as adjective cannot be plural).

**28.** A lunch of soup and sandwiches **(C) do** not appeal to all of the **(D) students**.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **does**. (Subject "lunch" is singular).

**29.** Some of us have to study their lessons more carefully if we expect to pass this examination.
*   **Answer: (B)** (The error is "their").
*   *Correction:* Should be **our**. (Agreement with "us/we").

**30.** Mr. Peters used to **(A) think** of **(B) hisself** as the only **(C) president** of the company.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **himself**.

**31.** The instructor advised the students **(A) for** the **(B) procedures** to follow in writing the term paper.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **on**. (Advise on something).

**32.** Although both of them are trying to get the scholarship, she has the **(D) highest** grades.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **higher**. (Comparative for two entities).

**33.** The new technique calls for **(A) heat** the mixture before **(B) applying** it to the wood.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **heating**. (Preposition + gerund).

**34.** The pilot and the crew distributed the life preservers **(C) between** the twenty frantic passengers.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **among**. (Three or more entities).

**35.** A **(A) five-thousand-dollars** reward was offered **(B) for** the capture of the escaped criminals.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **dollar**. (Noun as adjective is singular).

**36.** The **(A) equipment** in the office was badly **(B) in** need of **(D) to be repaired**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **repair**. (In need of + noun).

**37.** A liter is one of the metric measurements, **(D) aren’t they**?
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **isn't it**. ("Liter" is singular).

**38.** We thought he is planning to go on vacation after **(C) the first** of the month.
*   **Answer: (A)** (Note: The error corresponds to the verb "is").
*   *Correction:* Should be **was**. (Sequence of tenses: thought... was).

**39.** There **(A) are** a large supply of pens and notebooks in the storeroom to the left of the library entrance.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **is**. (Subject "supply" is singular).

**40.** The president refuses to accept **(A) either** of the four new proposals made by the contractors.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **any**. ("Either" is for two items).

### **Practice Test 3: Structure and Written Expression (Part B)**

**16.** She wishes that we **(A) didn’t send** her the candy yesterday because she’s **(C) on** a diet.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **hadn’t sent**. (Past wish).

**17.** They are **(A) planning on** attending the convention **(C) next** month, and **(D) so I am**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **so am I**.

**18.** Today was **(A) such** beautiful day that I couldn’t bring **(B) myself** to complete all my chores.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **such a**. (Such + a + adjective).

**19.** While they were away at the beach, they allowed **(D) their neighbors use** their barbeque grill.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **to use**. (Allow + obj + infinitive).

**20.** The artist tried **(A) stimulate** interest in painting by **(B) taking** his students to the museums.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **to stimulate**.

**21.** Mumps **(A) are** a very **(B) common** disease which usually **(D) affects** children.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **is**. ("Mumps" is singular non-count).

**22.** Nancy said **(A) that** she **(B) went** to the supermarket before coming home.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **had gone**. (Past perfect for previous action).

**23.** **(A) Before** she moved here, Arlene had been president of the organization **(C) since** four years.
*   **Answer: (C)** (The letter is likely D in the key based on position, text says C/D area).
*   *Correction:* Should be **for**. (For + duration).

**24.** Each of the nurses **(B) report** to the operating room when his or her name is called.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **reports**. ("Each" is singular).

**25.** The **(A) athlete**, together with his coach and several relatives, **(D) are** traveling to the Olympic Games.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **is**. (Subject "athlete" is singular).

**26.** Professor Duncan teaches **(B) both** anthropology **(C) as well as** sociology each fall.
*   **Answer: (C)** (Error is the combination).
*   *Correction:* Should be **and** (after "both") OR remove "both".

**27.** My brother is in California on vacation, but I wish he **(C) was** here so that he could help me repair my car.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **were**. (Present wish).

**28.** I certainly appreciate **(B) him** telling us about the delay in delivering the materials...
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **his**. (Possessive before gerund).

**29.** The chemistry instructor explained the experiment in **(B) such of a** way that it was easily understood.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **such a**.

**30.** Rudolph Nureyev has become one of the **(B) greatest dancer** that the ballet world has ever known.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **dancers**. (One of the + plural).

**31.** He has **(A) less** friends in his classes now **(B) than** he had last year.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **fewer**. ("Friends" is count).

**32.** The town we visited was a **(A) four-days** journey from our hotel, so we took the train instead of the bus.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **four-day**. (Adjective cannot be plural).

**33.** The influence of the nation’s literature, art, and science **(C) have** captured widespread attention.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **has**. (Subject "influence" is singular).

**34.** The leader emphasized the need for justice and equality **(C) between** his people.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **among**. (More than two).

**35.** **(A) Many** of the population in the rural areas is composed of manual laborers.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **Much**. ("Population" is non-count here).

**36.** Several people have **(A) apparent** tried to change the man’s mind, but he refuses to listen.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **apparently**. (Adverb modifies verb).

**37.** Keith is one of the **(C) most intelligent boys** of the science class.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **in**. (Use "in" after superlative for places/groups).

**38.** The girls were sorry **(B) to had missed** the singers when they arrived at the airport.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **to have missed**. (Perfect infinitive).

**39.** When Keith visited Alaska, he lived in **(B) a** igloo in the winter months as well as in the spring.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **an**. (Vowel sound).

**40.** The harder he tried, the **(B) worst** he danced before the large audience.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **worse**. (Double comparative).

### **Practice Test 4: Structure and Written Expression (Part B)**

**16.** While searching for the wreckage of **(B) a** unidentified aircraft, the Coast Guard encountered severe squalls at sea.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **an**.

**17.** Although a number of police officers **(A) was** guarding the priceless treasures in the museum...

**20.** While verbalization is the most common form of language in existence, humans make use of many **(C) others** systems and techniques...
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **other**. (Adjective).

**21.** The need for a well-rounded education was an idea espoused by the Greeks **(D) in time** of Socrates.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **in the time**. (Specific time).

**22.** Writers and media personnel sell **(B) theirselves** best by the impression given in their verbal expression.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **themselves**.

**23.** In the spirit of the naturalist writers, that author’s work portrays man’s struggle for **(D) surviving**.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **survival**. (Noun).

**24.** Stephen Crane’s story is a clinical portrayal of man as an animal trapped by **(C) the** fear and hunger.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Remove **the**. (Indefinite/abstract nouns).

**25.** Their silly, whiny conversation **(A) on a child level** was meant to create tension and heighten Nancy’s fears and anxiety.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **on a child's level** or **childish**.

**26.** For a long time, **(A) this** officials have been known throughout the country as political bosses and law enforcers.

*   *Correction:* Should be **these**. (Plural).

**27.** Nora **(A) hardly never** misses an opportunity to play in the tennis tournaments.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **hardly ever**. (Double negative).

**28.** Air pollution, together with littering, **(B) are** causing many problems in our large, industrial cities today.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **is**. (Subject "Air pollution" is singular).

**29.** Because of the severe snow storm and the road blocks, the air force dropped food and medical supplies **(D) close** the city.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **close to**.

**30.** Hummingbirds are the only birds **(B) capable to fly** backward as well as forward, up, and down.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **capable of flying**.

**31.** The news of the president’s treaty negotiations with the foreign government **(B) were** received with mixed emotions...
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **was**. ("News" is singular).

**32.** Angie’s bilingual ability and previous experience were the qualities **(B) that which** helped her get the job...
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **that** OR **which**. (Redundant).

**33.** **(A) Joel** giving up smoking has caused him to gain weight and become irritable with his acquaintances.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **Joel's**. (Possessive before gerund).

**34.** They asked me **(A) what did happen** last night, but I was unable to tell them.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **what happened**. (Embedded question).

**35.** The test administrator ordered **(B) we** not to open our books until he told us to do so.
*   **Answer: (B)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **us**. (Object pronoun).

**36.** Our new neighbors had been living in Arizona **(C) since** ten years before moving to their present house.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **for**. (Duration).

**37.** I **(A) would of** attended the meeting of the planning committee last week, but I had to deliver a speech...
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **would have**.

**38.** We are **(A) suppose** to read all of chapter seven and answer the questions for tomorrow’s class.
*   **Answer: (A)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **supposed**.

**39.** The explanation that our instructor gave us was different **(C) than** the one yours gave you.
*   **Answer: (C)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **from**.

**40.** In the sixteenth century, Spain became involved in foreign wars... and could not find the means **(D) of finance** the battles that ensued.
*   **Answer: (D)**
*   *Correction:* Should be **of financing**.

### **Practice Test 6: Structure and Written Expression (Part B)**

**16.** Most Americans would not be happy without a color television, two cars, and **(D) working at** an extra job.

**17.** The lion has long been a symbol of strength, power, and **(D) it is very cruel**.

**19.** Nobody had known before the presentation that Sue and her sister **(C) will receive** the awards for outstanding scholarship.

**20.** In 1927, Charles Lindbergh was the first to fly solo nonstop from New York to Paris in **(D) such** short time.

**21.** Until his last class at the university in 1978, Bob always **(B) turns** in all of his assignments on time.

**22.** When I last saw Janet, she **(B) hurried** to her next class on the other side of the campus...
